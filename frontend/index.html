How Chikitsak AI Works

A transparent, 4-step process combining supervised machine learning with responsible generative AI explanations.

01

Select Category

Choose the relevant health category matching what you are experiencing to avoid overwhelming symptom lists.

02

Answer Questions

Answer simple Yes/No questions regarding specific symptoms and clinical risk factors.

03

AI Analysis

The selected symptoms are processed by the trained Support Vector Machine model against 132 features.

04

Explanation

Gemini provides a simple, structured explanation of the model's prediction in clear patient-friendly language.

Architecture & Guardrails

Built with Ethical AI Guardrails

Chikitsak AI separates predictive statistical inference from patient communication. Gemini never diagnoses independently—its role is strictly to interpret the ML model's prediction clearly and empathetically.

SVM Classifier Pipeline

Trained on clinical datasets encompassing 132 distinct symptom features across 41 conditions. It performs non-linear boundary classification to identify statistical correlations between reported symptoms.

Google Gemini Explanation

Acts solely as an empathetic medical communicator. Adheres to strict ethical boundaries: never recommends drug dosages, never proclaims definitive medical certainty, and reminds patients to seek human physicians.
